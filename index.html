What is resume screening?

Screening is the first filter in hiring: every resume is read against the role. Doing it by hand is slow, so HireLens uses keyword matching and weighted scoring. Read the step-by-step guide to see how a score is built.

Every score comes with a breakdown, so recruiters can see why a candidate ranked where they did.
