How it works

How HireLens works

A recruiter pastes a job description and uploads resumes. HireLens reads each resume, finds the skills the job asks for, and turns the match into a score out of 100.

Video: a 2-minute walkthrough

Audio: listen to the summary

Screening steps

  1. Upload resumes
  2. Paste the job description
  3. Match keywords
  4. Rank candidates

Criteria we check

  • Skills
  • Experience
  • Education
  • Formatting

Scoring weights

Scoring weights by criterion
Criterion Weight What it checks
Skills 50% Keywords from the job description found in the resume
Experience 25% Years and roles that match the job
Education 15% Degree and field required by the job
Formatting 10% Clear sections that a parser can read
